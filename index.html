Biography

Music as a time machine.

Aura Vibin makes music the way some people keep a journal — except this one has bass in it. Montréal-based, she writes hip-hop/R&B built on deep, body-feeling 808s, sharp wordplay, and atmosphere you can sink into.

Sometimes it's shadow work. Sometimes it's just play. Mostly it's both at once — connecting dots, clearing her head, finding the exact words for feelings that usually go unnamed, and having a good time doing it.

Her first album, On a Cloud, came out under the name Aura. Exposé is next. It's the vibrations, baby.

The Sound

808s, wordplay, atmosphere.

Technical bars over atmospheric melodies. Deep, body-feeling low end. Harmonies that feel like messages coming through.
